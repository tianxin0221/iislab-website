Ir Dr. Ming Li 李明

Assistant Professor · Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering

Dr. Ming Li leads IIS Lab at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. His research spans blockchain, cyber-physical systems, large language models, and their applications in industry.

He encourages students to engage with real industrial challenges and cultivates a collaborative team environment where rigorous discussion and creative thinking go hand in hand.
